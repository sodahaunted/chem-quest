Welcome to Chem-quest!

ChemQuest is a small website made to help students with high-school level chemistry. With the recent developments of generative AI, human-made resources are becoming more difficult to find. ChemQuest aims to stand as a reliable, easy-to-understand resource for students struggling with chemistry.

Created for chem students, by a chem student. This site was inspired by study guide docs shared among my friends during ALP classes, as well as retro note-taking apps from the 2010s.
